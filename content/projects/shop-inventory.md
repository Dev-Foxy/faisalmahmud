## The brief

FarmtechBD needed one place to manage products, suppliers, sales and stock across their business — replacing scattered spreadsheets.

## What I built

- Product, supplier and customer management with batch and expiry tracking
- Trading flows: sales, purchases, invoices, returns and exchanges
- Stock and expiry alerts
- Reports for sales, purchases, inventory and cash flow
- Role-based user management
