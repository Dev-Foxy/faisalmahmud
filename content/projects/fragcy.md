## The brief

Fragcy needed an online store that felt as premium as the fragrances it sells — and that worked smoothly for customers paying with local mobile wallets.

## What I built

- A custom, mobile-first storefront designed from scratch in Figma
- A fluid product discovery flow with categories and new-arrival carousels
- Secure **bKash** payment integration
- A Firebase backend for products, orders and customers
